# SYNTH Brand Foundation V1

## Canonical Name

`SYNTH`

Always render the public-facing brand name in all caps unless a specific UI size
constraint requires a small-caps or wordmark treatment derived from the approved
logo system.

## Canonical Subtitle

`Cybernetic Zen Master with Market Data`

This subtitle is brand language only. It is not a technical capability claim, a
regulatory statement, or a promise of autonomous market behavior.

## Approved Visual Direction

The approved visual direction is the provided Synth logo concept sheet:

- primary symbol: angular interlocked `S` monogram
- construction feel: beveled, metallic, cybernetic, engineered
- accent feel: internal violet / purple luminous energy
- presentation field: dark background with high contrast symbol lighting
- wordmark feel: geometric, wide-tracked, futuristic sans serif

This concept sheet is the visual source of truth for the first canonical Synth
branding foundation. Future refinements must stay recognizably inside this
direction unless a new brand version explicitly replaces it.

## Brand Meaning

The brand should communicate:

- synthetic intelligence applied to market structure
- disciplined, calm, deliberate operation rather than hype
- engineered precision with a restrained, almost meditative posture
- a system that observes, maps, and reviews markets rather than theatrically
  “predicting everything”

Public wording should emphasize:

- market structure
- review
- mapping
- context
- measured automation boundaries

Avoid public wording that implies:

- omniscience
- guaranteed outcomes
- mystical trading powers
- unrestricted autonomous execution

## Visual Identity

Core identity parts:

- monogram: the interlocked `S`
- wordmark: `SYNTH`
- optional subtitle: `Cybernetic Zen Master with Market Data`

Visual traits to preserve:

- strong angular silhouette
- high recognizability of the `S` shape without relying on glow
- metallic or solid engineered mass in hero usage
- violet accent reserved as an energy highlight, not as the only defining shape
- generous negative space around the monogram
- dark-first hero presentation, with light and monochrome alternatives supported

Do not drift into:

- soft organic curves that weaken the engineered `S`
- neon overload that makes the symbol unreadable
- thin-line only renditions that collapse at small sizes
- a generic crypto-logo look with no durable silhouette

## Variant Roles

### Master Variant

Use for:

- primary product pages
- landing pages
- brand documentation
- keynote / hero surfaces
- social headers or polished presentation surfaces

Definition:

- metallic / dimensional `S` monogram
- violet internal accent lighting
- canonical `SYNTH` wordmark
- optional subtitle only where space and context support it

The master variant is the highest-expression brand form, not the default for
small UI elements.

### Accent Variant

Use for:

- highlighted product surfaces
- featured dashboard headers
- splash screens
- campaign or narrative brand moments

Definition:

- same canonical `S` silhouette
- stronger violet accent or glow treatment
- may reduce metallic shading if clarity improves

Rules:

- accent color supports the shape; it must not replace legibility
- accent variant must still be recognizable when glow is reduced or removed

### Monochrome Variant

Use for:

- favicon source simplification
- print
- watermarking
- low-ink or laser output
- accessibility fallbacks
- constrained embedded surfaces

Definition:

- single-color `S` monogram and compatible wordmark
- black-on-light or white-on-dark execution
- no dependence on bevel, texture, or glow for recognition

Monochrome recognizability is mandatory. If the mark fails in one flat color,
the design is not production-ready.

## Subtitle Usage Rules

Use the subtitle only when:

- brand introduction is helpful
- enough horizontal or vertical space exists
- the surface is editorial, marketing, overview, or brand-facing

Do not use the subtitle:

- in favicons
- in narrow mobile headers
- in compact dashboard nav
- in dense runtime tables
- in places where it could read as a technical or legal claim

Subtitle formatting rules:

- keep exact casing: `Cybernetic Zen Master with Market Data`
- do not paraphrase in logo lockups
- keep it visually subordinate to `SYNTH`
- avoid over-tight tracking that harms readability

## Favicon And Small-Size Requirements

Favicons and tiny marks must use a simplified symbol-only asset derived from the
canonical `S` monogram.

Rules:

- do not use the full composite concept sheet as a favicon
- do not include the subtitle in favicon or app-icon sizes
- prefer symbol-only for 16x16, 24x24, and 32x32
- simplify inner bevel/glow detail where needed
- test recognizability on dark and light browser tabs
- ensure the shape remains readable at 1x and 2x pixel densities

If the full monogram is too detailed for tiny sizes, a reduced small-size
version of the same `S` silhouette should be created rather than inventing a
different logo.

## Accessibility And Contrast Guidance

Minimum guidance:

- maintain strong figure-ground contrast on both dark and light placements
- never rely on purple glow alone for edge definition
- ensure wordmark contrast is readable against the background
- preserve a clearly visible silhouette in grayscale screenshots
- test monochrome and low-brightness rendering

For UI use:

- avoid placing the mark over noisy charts without a backing plate or spacing
- provide sufficient quiet space around the mark
- use flat monochrome assets where detailed metallic shading reduces clarity

## Public-Facing Wording Vs Internal Lore

Public-facing wording:

- SYNTH
- market data
- mapping
- context
- review
- research
- read-only dashboards

Internal lore may reference:

- cybernetic calm
- zen posture
- system discipline
- symbolic narrative framing

Rules:

- internal lore must not replace plain public explanation
- internal lore must not appear as technical documentation or capability claims
- legal, operational, and product surfaces should prefer plain language first

## Implementation Guidance

For `assets/brand/synth/`:

- keep source assets versioned and named by variant
- separate hero/master art from favicon-ready simplifications
- include export guidance for dark, light, and monochrome use

This brand foundation changes no trading, runtime, decision, execution, broker,
or dashboard architecture. It is a documentation-only branding baseline.
