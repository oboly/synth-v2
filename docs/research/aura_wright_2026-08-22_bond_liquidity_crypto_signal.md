## Source claims worth preserving

Aura's framework identifies two high-pressure windows:

- 2026-08-20: Mars-Pluto, described as rapid/explosive systemic force and a release of accumulated leverage pressure.
- 2026-08-24/25: Saturn hard aspect to a proprietary wealth point, described as debt, limits, consequences, yield pressure, and reckoning.

She connects the August window to:

- Japanese government-bond stress;
- threatened yen carry-trade unwinding;
- U.S. long-end Treasury stress;
- Treasury bond-buyback/liquidity support;
- weaker confidence in debt/currency purchasing power;
- BTC/gold demand as scarce-asset or debasement trades.

Her stated macro thesis is that the pressure dates are not uniformly risk-off for crypto. They may damage the leveraged debt system while strengthening the case for scarce assets.

## Important correction / normalization

Treasury buybacks are not equivalent to Federal Reserve QE or literal money printing. The useful market signal is therefore not `buyback = new money`, but:

```text
long-end sovereign stress
-> official liquidity/market-function intervention
-> market expectation of further accommodation / financial repression / currency debasement risk
-> demand for scarce assets
```

Treat the direct causal claim as a hypothesis, not a fact.

## Synth market hypothesis

The more useful account-agnostic model is two-stage:

```text
Stage 1: funding/carry stress
JGB/UST stress -> deleveraging -> possible risk-asset drawdown

Stage 2: policy/liquidity response
intervention/accommodation expectations -> weaker fiat confidence / debasement trade -> BTC/gold relative strength
```

A yen carry unwind is therefore not automatically bullish BTC on impact. The strongest regime evidence would be BTC absorbing or rapidly recovering from a macro deleveraging shock while sovereign-bond stress and liquidity intervention rise.
