## Overall interpretation

The batch expresses a broadly bullish four-week view. Every expected return and every lower bound is positive. That makes the dataset useful as a directional hypothesis, but also indicates a strong bullish-response bias.

The readings concentrate the strongest upside expectations in smaller or higher-beta assets, while BTC, ETH and several established DeFi assets receive more moderate forecasts. The implied pattern is therefore not simply "the whole market rises equally". It resembles a risk-on rotation in which selected infrastructure, AI, oracle and ecosystem tokens outperform the larger market.

The source-provided confidence values mostly range from 70% to 88%. These figures are provenance data from Aplus, not independently calibrated probabilities.
